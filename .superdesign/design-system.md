# bOden GeoDIN Fusion Design System

GeoDIN Fusion is a professional office command center for experienced GeoDIN users. Preserve the bOden identity: condensed technical typography, black structural lines, warm paper surfaces, restrained amber accents and green validation states.

## Principles

1. Dense but legible: maximize usable data space without decorative emptiness.
2. Three-step workflow: sources → selection/analysis → master and conflict handling.
3. Persistent control: progress and final actions remain visible while large tables scroll independently.
4. Internal overlays: confirmations, errors, progress and updates use branded in-app modals/toasts—never native browser alerts or uncontrolled Windows message boxes.
5. Touch and mouse: all important targets are at least 40px high while rows remain information-dense.

## Geometry

- Header: 64px
- Persistent footer: 86px
- Main: remaining viewport height
- Three-column grid: 330px / flexible / 330px, tightened below 1200px
- Panel border: 2px square
- Buttons: 7px radius
- Central point viewport must own its vertical scrollbar; header remains sticky.
